NEON STICK FIGHT

A temp fights their way from day one to the corner office. A free stick-figure fighting game you can play right here, on your phone, or online against a coworker. Click the game, then press Enter.

THE STORY

FROM TEMP TO THE TOP

You're the TEMP. First day, borrowed badge, no desk, and a building full of people who've already decided you won't last the week. The only way up is through them.

Every floor of the tower teaches you something about office life. The INTERN swings at everything because nobody told them not to. HELPDESK waits for you to make the first mistake, then closes your ticket. The COURIER won't slow down for anybody, SECURITY decides who belongs in the building, and the JANITOR knows the floors nobody else goes to. At the top, THE MANAGER has your performance review ready, and it's now.

Surviving here works the same way as surviving a fight. Let the loud ones overcommit. Don't burn yourself out trying to look busy. When somebody finally swings too hard, make it cost them. Panic and everyone sees it. Make it all the way up without asking for a second chance, and you'll find out who signs THE MANAGER's paychecks.

LIFE IN THE TOWER

THE CLIMB

floors up the building, best of three rounds (or one, or five: your call), against coworkers who each fight their own way: the turtle, the rusher, the grappler. Beat the boss without a continue and you'll find out who else is upstairs.

COWORKERS

From the TEMP on day one to the corner office: the RECEPTIONIST who puts you on hold, the LAWYER who objects, and HR, who's writing all of this down. Each one has their own moves and a file on their page.

HORDE FLOORS

Restructuring day. Wave after wave of temps come through the office doors after your spot. Clear the floor, vault the stairwell railing, and do it again one floor down. See how deep you get.

OFFICE RIVALRIES

Settle it with the coworker in the next cubicle. Send a four-letter code for a quick match, or set up a crew room for the whole department, with standings, head-to-head records and winner stays on.

OFFICE POLITICS

Panic in front of everyone and it shows: you overbalance, spin out, trip over your own feet. A few mistakes, made at exactly the wrong moment, turn out to be brilliant. Nobody's going to tell you which.

HOW TO PLAY

1LEFT PUNCHjab · key U
2RIGHT PUNCHcross · key I
3LEFT KICKsnap kick · key J
4RIGHT KICKroundhouse · key K

One button per limb and a direction. That's all the controls there are. The rest is reading the person across from you.

PLAY A FRIEND ONLINE

No accounts and nothing to download. From the main menu pick VERSUS ONLINE, choose your fighter, and then one of these.

QUICK MATCH

  1. One of you picks HOST A GAME and gets a four-letter room code and a link.
  2. Send the link, or read the code out loud. Codes skip 0, O, 1, I and L so nobody mixes them up.
  3. The other opens the link, or picks JOIN A GAME and types the code.

When a match ends, REMATCH keeps the same fighters and NEW CHARACTERS goes back to the pick. The room keeps a running tally until somebody leaves.

CREW ROOMS

  1. Pick CREATE CREW ROOM and name your crew. You get a link that stays good as long as people keep using it.
  2. Drop it in the group chat. Everybody who opens it lands in the crew's lobby.
  3. Press PLAY when you're ready. The next two ready players fight, and the winner stays on.

The lobby keeps standings for the whole crew: wins, losses, your record against each person, and everyone's favorite fighter.

WHY IT FEELS LOCAL

Online fights use rollback netcode. Each side plays its own input right away, guesses the other's, and quietly rewinds and replays the last few frames when the guess was wrong. The connection goes peer to peer when it can, and through Cloudflare when two phones can't reach each other.

Want to keep your crews on a new phone? PROFILE in the online menu saves them to your email.
