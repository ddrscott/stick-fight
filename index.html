WHAT'S IN THE BOX

FIGHTERS

From the TEMP on day one to the corner office. Every fighter has their own stats, body, unique moves and strings, and a full move sheet below.

ARCADE LADDER

Six floors up the building, best of three, against CPUs that get tougher and fight their own way: the turtle, the rusher, the grappler. Beat the boss without a continue and someone is waiting upstairs.

HORDE MODE

Wave after wave of goons through the office doors. Clear the floor, vault the stairwell railing, and do it again one floor down.

ONLINE VERSUS

Send a friend a four-letter room code or a link. A crew room is a permanent link for your group, with standings, head to head records and winner stays on. Rematch in one press.

ROLLBACK NETCODE

Both machines run the whole fight and quietly fix their guesses, the way the big fighting games do it, peer to peer whenever the two phones can reach each other.

BOTCHES AND SECRETS

Mash and you'll mess up: overbalance, spin out, trip over your own feet. A few mistimed presses, in just the right window, come out as something much better. We're not saying which.

PHONES AND CONTROLLERS

Touch controls with a floating stick and four arcade buttons, any standard gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo), or the keyboard.

INSTALLABLE

Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen in landscape, starts fast and plays offline. Updates show up on their own.

HOW TO PLAY

One button per limb, like Tekken, from your fighter's own point of view.

1LEFT PUNCHjab · key U
2RIGHT PUNCHcross · key I
3LEFT KICKsnap kick · key J
4RIGHT KICKroundhouse · key K
MOVE
A and D or the arrows. W jumps, S crouches, Shift (or a double tap) runs.
GUARD
Hold back, away from the other fighter. Standing guard stops highs and mids; lows go under it, so crouch and hold back for those.
THROW
1+3 or 2+4. Throws beat a guard but whiff on a crouch. Press the same buttons while grabbed to break it.
STRINGS
Press in rhythm and the hits chain: 1, 2, 3, 4 is the Neon Tornado. Every fighter's strings are on their page.
STAMINA
The thin bar under health is your attack budget. Spend the last point and you faint for a beat. It refills fast when you stop swinging.
PAD AND TOUCH
On a gamepad X, Y, A, B are 1, 2, 3, 4, L1 guards and R1 throws. On a phone, a floating stick on the left and the four buttons on the right.
